FAQ

Common questions.

How do I get a reply?

On the main screen, upload a screenshot. You can also choose Paste text or Build it yourself. Add the conversation, choose a tone and generate your reply.

Which tones can I choose?

Charm AI includes Warm, Flirty, Funny, Bold, Sigma, Delulu and Nonchalant. Premium access may apply to some tones.

What can Chat Wrapped import?

Chat Wrapped supports exported chats from WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger. Choose the platform and follow the in-app export guide.

Why does the keyboard need Full Access?

Full Access lets the keyboard make the AI request you start and read the local premium status shared by the main app. Charm AI does not monitor keystrokes or make background AI requests.

Which profile tools are Premium?

The Profile Coach area for bio rewrites, photo feedback and personalized profile tips is labeled Premium inside the app.
